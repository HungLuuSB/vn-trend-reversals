The analysis code, the dated decision log, the configuration and metrics of every run, and the
non-performing-loan ratios transcribed from page images are openly available on Zenodo under an MIT
licence \citep{LuuPhamNgo2026Code}. The daily price data that support the findings of this study
are available from CafeF and Yahoo Finance. Restrictions apply to the availability of these data,
which are not redistributed with this article. The VIX and US Treasury yields are publicly
available from the Federal Reserve Bank of St.\ Louis (FRED), and the daily geopolitical risk index
from \citet{CaldaraIacoviello2022}. All reported results come from one frozen data snapshot
(identifier \gen{SnapshotId}); the snapshot, including the ratios read by optical character
recognition, is available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request. Each run records
the code commit that produced it, and the run records link every table to its run. Because
the price archives are not redistributed and the providers' files may have changed since they were
downloaded, a third party can rebuild the dataset from the original sources only approximately.
