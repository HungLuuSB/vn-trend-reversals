The analysis code, the dated decision record, the configuration of every run and the generator
of every number in this article are available from the corresponding author and will be released
in a public repository on publication.\todo{Authors: confirm the repository and its URL before
submission.} All results come from one frozen data snapshot (identifier \gen{SnapshotId}) and the
code at commit \gen{FinalCommit}. Daily prices are from CafeF and Yahoo Finance and cannot be
redistributed under those sources' terms; the code rebuilds the snapshot from them. The VIX and US
Treasury yields are public from the Federal Reserve Bank of St.\ Louis (FRED), and the daily
geopolitical risk index is public from \citet{CaldaraIacoviello2022}. Bank statements and news
articles are public on CafeF; the extracted non-performing-loan ratios are available from the
corresponding author on request.
