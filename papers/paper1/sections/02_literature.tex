\subsection{Duration dependence of trends and market phases}
\plan{Lunde \& Timmermann (2004): filter-defined bull/bear phases, discrete-time hazard, bull hazard falls / bear hazard rises with age, interest rates shift hazards (in-sample). Zakamulin (2023): two centuries, mostly positive duration dependence.}
\plan{gap $\rightarrow$ age-only hazards average over the distance to the filter trigger; index level; no out-of-sample test. Sets up our age-vs-distance result.}
\todo[inline]{Strand 1.}

\subsection{Survival analysis of price reversals and trading signals}
\plan{Yao, Partington \& Stevenson (2005): Cox models of daily index runs (mean \textasciitilde{}2.5 days), Brier score worse than random walk out of sample. Hu \& Zhou (2024): survival models of time to a +5\% target after technical signals, 380 S\&P 500 stocks, argue stocks cannot be pooled.}
\plan{gap $\rightarrow$ the unit (noisy one-day runs; target returns) and pooling across stocks; our volatility scaling makes swings comparable across stocks.}
\todo[inline]{Strand 2.}

\plan{gap $\rightarrow$ censoring of open trends; updating the prediction as a trend ages (landmarks).}
