\subsection{The Vietnamese stock market}\label{sec:market}

Vietnam has three trading venues. The Ho Chi Minh City Stock Exchange (HOSE) lists the largest
firms, including every VN30 constituent. The Hanoi Stock Exchange (HNX) lists smaller firms, and
UPCoM is a trading venue for registered but unlisted public companies. Individual investors account
for most trading value, and foreign investors for about a tenth \citep{SHS2025,HOSE2025}.

\paragraph{Daily price limits} Each stock may trade only within a band around its reference price.
The band has changed over the study period. HOSE allowed $\pm5\%$ until 14 January 2013 and $\pm7\%$
since; HNX allowed $\pm7\%$ and then $\pm10\%$ from the same date; UPCoM allowed $\pm10\%$ until 30
June 2015 and $\pm15\%$ since. On HOSE the reference price is the previous close. On UPCoM, and on
HNX before 29 July 2013, it was a volume-weighted average price. On ex-dates the reference is the
previous close divided by the corporate-action ratio. The ceiling is rounded down and the floor up to
the tick size in force. We encode every one of these rules as a dated schedule with its legal source
and compute the band for each stock-day. Where the reference was a volume-weighted average that our
data cannot reconstruct, the limit is recorded as unknown and is never guessed.

\paragraph{Settlement and short sales} Equity trades settled T+3 until the trades of 31 December
2015 and T+2 from 4 January 2016. Short selling of individual stocks is not generally available, so
the trading test in Section~\ref{sec:trading} is long-only.

\subsection{Universe and sample}\label{sec:universe}

The universe has two parts. The first is the VN30, HOSE's index of 30 large and liquid stocks, taken
point in time. A stock is in the universe from the effective date of its addition to the day before
the effective date of its removal, using every semi-annual review and ad hoc replacement since the
index started on 6 February 2012. Stocks that later left the index or were delisted stay in the
sample for the years they were members, which avoids survivorship bias. The reconstructed history
has \gen{VNThirtyChanges} changes involving \gen{VNThirtyStocks} stocks and holds exactly 30 members
on every session. It matches all \gen{VNThirtyBaskets} complete baskets printed in official or press
sources.

The second part is every Vietnamese bank whose shares traded on any of the three venues, for every
spell in which they traded: \gen{BankCount} banks, including banks that later merged or moved
venue. Banks registered only on UPCoM are kept and flagged; results are also reported without their
UPCoM days.

\paragraph{Study period} The sample runs from the first HOSE session of 2012, 3 January 2012, to the
close of 20 November 2026. Prices from October 2011 are used only to initialise trailing quantities
such as ATR20 and the swing state. Nothing after the 20 November 2026 close enters any feature,
label or outcome, and swings still open at that close are right-censored. The trading calendar is
the HOSE calendar.

\paragraph{Filters} A stock-day enters the risk set only if it is in the universe on that date, the
stock has at least 250 sessions of its own price history before it, and the stock is inside an open
swing. Table~\ref{tab:sample} reports the counts after each step.

\subsection{Price data}\label{sec:prices}

Daily open, high, low, close and volume come from the bulk end-of-day archives of CafeF, a
Vietnamese financial portal \todo{cite CafeF}. Every download is stored
unchanged before parsing. Where the bulk archive lacks a stock's history before a change of venue,
it is filled from CafeF's per-ticker history; on the stock-days both sources cover, the two agree on
all five fields on every day (\gen{PriceOverlapDays} stock-days). After the fill, the sample covers
\gen{CoverageVNThirty} of VN30 member-days and \gen{CoverageBanks} of bank-days. Two VN30 members
of 2012--2016 and one bank of 2012 have no usable history in either source; they are listed in
\ref{sec:appendix}.

\paragraph{Raw and adjusted prices} We keep raw prices and compute adjusted prices ourselves from
the corporate actions. Each disputed action (where our inferred ratio and a second source disagreed,
or no second source existed) was checked against exchange, depository or company notices, and the
adjustment ratio was computed from the verified facts by one formula. Adjusted prices are
back-adjusted, so every past price level depends on later corporate actions. They are therefore used
only for returns and scale-free ratios, such as ATR20 over price. Everything that depends on a price
level, such as the price bands and the fill checks of the trading test, uses raw prices.

\subsection{Global and local market data}\label{sec:global}

Block D uses fourteen series observed before the Vietnamese open (Table~\ref{tab:features}). US
equity indices, the dollar index, gold and Brent futures, the Hang Seng, the Shanghai Composite and
the exchange rates come from Yahoo Finance; the VIX and the US 10-year Treasury yield come from the
Federal Reserve Bank of St.\ Louis (FRED); and the daily geopolitical risk index comes from
\citet{CaldaraIacoviello2022}. We use the Shanghai Composite instead of the CSI 300 because the
source holds no CSI 300 history. Gold is the front-month COMEX future, and its return is set to
missing on contract-roll days. All raw downloads are archived with the time of download.

Block C uses the VN-Index and VN30 levels, the share of advancing stocks and market turnover, all from
the same price archive. Foreign net flows are left out of the main specification because they are
missing on \gen{ForeignMissingSessions} sessions, mostly between 2019 and 2021; a missing indicator
there would act as a dummy for the COVID-19 period. A robustness specification adds them with that
indicator.

\subsection{Point-in-time alignment}\label{sec:pit}

Every observation in the database carries the time at which it became public,
\texttt{available\_at}, stored in UTC. The decision for row $(i,t)$ is taken at 09:00 Vietnam time
(UTC+7) on the next HOSE session, and a feature may use a value only if it became public before that
moment. An automated test in the data pipeline fails if any feature row uses a later value.

The availability time follows each market's close in its own time zone, with daylight saving taken
from the time-zone database. US equity indices close at 16:00 New York time, which is 03:00 (US
summer) or 04:00 (US winter) in Vietnam on the next day, so they are usable for the Vietnamese session
that follows. The US 10-year yield is taken as public at 18:00 New York time, when the Treasury
posts its par yields. Daily exchange-rate bars are taken as public at the end of the London day. The
geopolitical risk index is updated weekly, so a day's value is taken as public only from the
Wednesday after the next Monday. When HOSE is closed while other markets trade, as during the Lunar
New Year, global features use the cumulative change since the last Vietnamese session.

\paragraph{Bank statements} The NPL ratio of a quarter becomes usable at 09:00 on the fifth session
after the later of the legal filing deadline and the creation date of the statement file. The
statements are those that CafeF lists today; the first release of each quarter is the earliest file
of the highest-ranked kind (consolidated before parent-only). Block E covers \gen{BlockECoverage}
bank-quarters, \gen{BlockEOCR} read by optical character recognition and \gen{BlockEVisual}
transcribed from page images.

\paragraph{News} News counts come from the archive of CafeF's news site, crawled in 2026, with each
article's publication time. A bank is mentioned when one of its unambiguous names appears in the
title, summary or body. Ambiguous short names are used only in a robustness check.

\paragraph{Leakage checks} Before the test years were used, we ran a perturbation test: all prices,
statements and news after a cutoff were scrambled or deleted, the features were rebuilt, and every
feature with an earlier decision time had to be unchanged. The test found two join errors in block E,
which we fixed; after the fix, no feature changed at any of the cutoffs tried.

\subsection{Disclosures about the data}\label{sec:datalimits}

Some choices could not be made perfectly point in time, and we state them here.
\begin{itemize}
\item The geopolitical risk index is a single 2026 vintage. Its values before about 2021 were not
  published in real time, so they are pseudo-real-time.
\item First releases are taken from the files CafeF lists today, and the news archive was crawled
  in 2026. A statement or article that was removed later cannot be recovered.
\item The pooled base rates of the labels were seen when the labels were built: for example, the
  share of rows whose swing reverses within five sessions. No model output for the test years was
  seen before the final run.
\item Swings of VN30 stocks are censored when the stock leaves the index. Exit can depend on recent
  performance, so this censoring may be informative.
\end{itemize}
