\section{Robustness}\label{sec:robust}
% Each check states what could break the main result and whether it does. All numbers from the
% final run (generated/numbers.tex).

\paragraph{Threshold size} A different $k$ changes the labels themselves, since swings are defined
by the threshold. Table~\ref{tab:k} repeats the main comparisons with $k=1.5$ and $k=3$, with labels
and features rebuilt from the same snapshot. Predictability against Kaplan--Meier holds for every
$k$, and it rises with $k$: larger thresholds give longer, smoother swings whose distance to the
threshold is more informative (C of \texttt{M}: \KOneFiveCM, \KTwoCM{} and \KThreeCM). The gain of
\texttt{ABCDx} over \texttt{M} shrinks as $k$ grows. At $k=1.5$, \texttt{ABCDx} has the higher C in
all \KOneFiveYearsAboveABCDxM{} test years, but its IBS interval still includes zero
(\KOneFiveDIBSABCDxM); at $k=3$ even the C interval includes zero (\KThreeDCABCDxM). The shuffled
controls stay at chance (at most \KOneFiveShuffledMax{} and \KThreeShuffledMax). The trading result
does not change: the model exit never beats the plain directional-change exit (Sharpe ratios
\KOneFiveSharpeModel{} against \KOneFiveSharpeDC{} at $k=1.5$, \KThreeSharpeModel{} against
\KThreeSharpeDC{} at $k=3$).

\paragraph{Bootstrap block length} Rows of one swing overlap for longer than 20 sessions. With
blocks of 40 and 60 sessions, the decisions on H1, H1-mech, H2 and H3, and the comparison of
\texttt{ABCx} with \texttt{M}, are unchanged (Appendix S1).

\paragraph{Censoring at the fold boundary} The purge drops training swings that end after the
training window. Censoring them at the boundary instead keeps their early rows. The main decisions
are unchanged: \texttt{ABCDx} beats Kaplan--Meier (\BoundaryABCDxKm), \texttt{ABCx} beats
\texttt{M} (\BoundaryABCxM), and \texttt{ABCDx} neither beats \texttt{M} (\BoundaryABCDxM) nor
\texttt{ABCx} (\BoundaryABCDxABCx).

\paragraph{Sub-periods and subsamples} Table~\ref{tab:slices} splits the test rows. \texttt{ABCDx}
beats Kaplan--Meier in \SlicesBeatKM{} of the \SlicesCount{} slices; the exception is 2022, a year of
steep market decline, when \texttt{M} ranked better than the full model.
\texttt{ABCDx} beats \texttt{M} under the decision rule in 2017--2019 and in 2020--2021, but not
in 2022 or 2023--2026. Results for banks and for other VN30 stocks are similar; dropping banks
registered only on UPCoM, or rows that closed at the price limit, changes nothing.

\paragraph{Foreign flows} Net foreign buying is missing for \ForeignMissingSessions{} sessions and
is left out of the main specification. Adding it with a missing indicator makes the IBS worse
($\Delta$IBS \DIBSABCDxFfABCDx).

\paragraph{Trading frictions} The trading result does not depend on the execution price or the
cost level (Table~\ref{tab:trading}). Forbidding trades on days whose price limit cannot be
computed leaves the model exit below the directional-change exit (Sharpe ratios \BtOpenSharpeModelSkip{}
against \BtOpenSharpeDcSkip), and so does assuming that a sell at a close on the floor never fills
(\BtOpenSharpeModelFloor{} against \BtOpenSharpeDcFloor).
